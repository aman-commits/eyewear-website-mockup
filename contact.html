Frequently Addressed Questions

Can I purchase prescription lenses for any frame in the catalogue? +

Yes. All optical frames and most sunglasses in our catalogue can be fitted with custom single-vision, progressive, or reading prescription lenses through our certified Zeiss and Essilor laboratory partnerships.

How does the private boutique fitting work? +

Upon reserving an appointment, our master opticians prepare the requested frames along with personalized curator alternatives. During your 45-minute private session, we calibrate bridge fit, temple curve, and pantoscopic angle for absolute comfort.

Are all frames in the catalogue authentic? +

L'Optique is an authorized stockist for Ray-Ban, Persol, Oliver Peoples, Oakley, and Vogue Eyewear. Every piece comes with the official certificate of origin, serial numbers, and manufacturer warranty.

What is the purpose of the WhatsApp Concierge action? +

Rather than impersonal automated checkouts, our clients receive personalized white-glove advisory. The WhatsApp action opens an instant chat with our atelier concierge, automatically referencing your chosen model, SKU, and availability.
