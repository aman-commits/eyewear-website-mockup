Artisanal Mastery

The Alchemy of Italian Acetate

True luxury begins deep in the hills of northern Italy, where cotton cellulose is cured for months before being pressed into solid color sheets. Each frame is sliced using diamond-tipped cutters, hand-tumbled in aged beechwood tumbling barrels with natural pumice, and buffed by master artisans for over three days.

Every contour, hinge barrel, and bridge bevel is checked against precise millimeter tolerances to achieve featherweight balance and generational endurance.
